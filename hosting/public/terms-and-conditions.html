SecretGuard helps identify and mask possible credentials in supported chat text inputs. Detection is not complete, and choosing Mask activates the website’s send control. You remain responsible for the information you share.

1. What SecretGuard provides

SecretGuard checks recognized text prompts locally on supported ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, Meta AI, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, and Mistral websites. When it identifies possible secrets, it offers Send, Edit, and Mask, or Edit and Mask when that protection mode is selected.

Send uses the original draft. Edit returns to the draft without sending. Mask replaces detected values with xxxxxxxxxx and attempts to send the masked draft. The extension also provides custom field names, local activity totals, and local unmasked-send History.

2. Your responsibilities

Use SecretGuard only with information and accounts you are authorized to access. Follow applicable laws, your organization’s security rules, and the terms of the chat services you use. Review your prompts and the detected values before choosing an action. Do not use real credentials when testing or reporting a problem.

If a credential is shared, revoke or rotate it through its issuer. Masking later, deleting local History, or uninstalling SecretGuard does not recall messages already shared with a chat service.

3. Coverage and availability

SecretGuard can miss secrets or flag text that is not confidential. It does not verify that a credential is valid. Protection depends on supported website inputs and send controls; website changes, browser restrictions, other extensions, or account requirements may affect it. An activated send control does not confirm that a provider received a message.

Files, images, voice input, native applications, and existing chat conversations are outside its scanning coverage. Chat websites may read text in their pages before you send it. SecretGuard does not replace a secrets manager, credential rotation, organizational controls, or a security review.

4. Local data and History

The Privacy Policy explains data handling. When you choose Send with detected secrets left unmasked and the website’s send control is activated, the complete original prompt, including the secrets, is saved on this device for up to 90 days. SecretGuard does not encrypt that History. Delete entries you no longer need and protect access to your browser profile.

You can delete individual entries or clear History. Hunasys cannot recover deleted local data or remotely remove it from your device. Clearing History does not reset activity totals or delete messages at a chat provider.
